Detector Triage

A rough prototype for finding where diversity detectors go wrong. Three linked views: the overview compares the detectors; click one of its cells to list that detector's histories in the matrix, where each row is one consumption history and each column one step (0-based); click a row to open its history. Synthetic histories have a known change point, so you can check yourself against the truth. MF histories have none: only disagreements are certain to contain an error, so agreeing groups are sorted to put the likeliest shared errors first, and a random audit sample estimates how many there are.
